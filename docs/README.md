# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome! This README is your entrypoint to project management processes at OctoAcme. Browse the links below to access detailed guides, templates, and standards used by the team.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows an iterative, stakeholder-focused project management approach designed to deliver customer value through clear phases and collaborative teamwork. The lifecycle progresses through **Initiation** (defining problems and stakeholders), **Planning** (scoping work and setting milestones), **Execution** (building and testing), **Release** (deploying and verifying), and **Retrospective/Continuous Improvement** (capturing learnings and iterating). This approach emphasizes small, testable increments and data-informed decisions.

Our core team roles include the **Project Manager (PM)**, who coordinates delivery, schedules, and communications; the **Product Manager/Product Lead (PdM)**, who defines outcomes and prioritizes the backlog; **Developers**, who implement features and collaborate on design; **QA/Testing**, who validate quality and acceptance criteria; and **Stakeholders/Sponsors**, who provide inputs and approvals. Each role works together to ensure projects deliver on time and meet customer needs.

Communication is central to our process. Teams hold **daily standups** and **weekly delivery syncs** to track progress and surface blockers, while **demos** at the end of each sprint or milestone showcase work to stakeholders. Stakeholder updates follow a standard status template covering progress, next steps, risks, and decisions needed. Escalations follow a clear path: **Team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor**, with security incidents routed through the on-call security team.

Quality assurance is built into every stage. The **PR workflow** includes CI automation that runs tests, linting, and security scans before code review. At least one approval is required before merging. Testing includes **unit tests** for new logic, **integration tests** where applicable, and **end-to-end smoke tests** for critical flows before release. Manual QA is conducted as needed for feature acceptance. Each release follows a standardized checklist, and rollback procedures and incident playbooks are maintained to handle production issues swiftly and systematically.
